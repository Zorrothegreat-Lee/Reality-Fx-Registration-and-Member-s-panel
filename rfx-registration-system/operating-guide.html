01

The Front Desk — where it all begins

The Front Desk is the main front of the business — the first thing anyone sees when they look up Reality FX. It is not just a brochure; it is where the journey is started.

What the Front Desk does

It presents the Academy — the courses, the prices, the promise — and lets someone enrol. The moment a course is paid for and the payment is approved (for example through PayPal), the system takes over automatically. It creates the student's enrolment record, generates an official invoice, and emails it.

Right after the purchase

The new student immediately receives two branded emails: the invoice confirming their purchase and ownership, and a secure registration link — a unique, time-limited link that carries them into the next room: the gate that leads to the Student Portal.

02

The Gate — nobody walks in unregistered

Buying a course is not the same as becoming a student. Between the Front Desk and the Portal stands a registration gate — deliberately, so we know every single person before they touch Academy material.

1

Secure link

The registration email's link is unique, single-use and time-limited. An expired or used link is refused and a fresh one is issued.

2

Identity

Name, surname, email, country, phone, address, date of birth — and a verification selfie, captured honestly from camera, mobile or files.

3

Human check

Email verification with a 6-digit code, plus CAPTCHA. A machine confirms the basics — a person stays part of the review.

4

Agreements

The exact version of the Terms, Conditions and Fair Usage Policy is recorded, with the exact time of acceptance.

5

Approval

The system verifies the registration is tied to a genuine paid enrolment. Staff approve — then a Student ID and Student Code are created.

6

Handshake

The approved identity is handed to RFX OS. RFX OS confirms receipt — idempotently, so a network hiccup never creates two identities for one student.

The gate is the security. Until this process completes, the student holds a demo / under-review profile — never full access. Registration links are tracked, verification codes are brute-force protected, and a failed registration can be re-applied for or resolved to credit or refund — all automated, all recorded.

04

RFX OS Academy — the classroom

RFX OS is the Academy itself: lessons, progress, assessments, certificates — the digital home of Reality FX education. It is the most protected room in the building, because it holds the value of the education.

Authorization, not just URLs

Possessing a lesson URL grants nothing. Every protected resource asks the same question: “Is this authenticated Student ID authorised to access this?” Only a yes delivers content. A student of Course A simply cannot reach Course B's material.

Content that stays yours

Pages are watermarked with the student's ID, text selection is blocked, printing is blacked out by default, and capture attempts are logged to the moderator. Print access is a privilege earned by trusted students — not a default right.
