01 The Three Rooms

Reality FX runs as one journey through three rooms. Each room has a specific purpose, and every student follows the same path.

Front Desk → Student Portal → RFX OS Academy
01
The Front Desk
The website · Where the world meets us
The Front Desk is the main front of the business — the first thing anyone sees. It presents the Academy: the courses, the prices, the promise. And it lets someone enrol.
  • Course pages show what the Academy teaches and what each pathway costs
  • Enrolment starts the moment a course is paid for and the payment is approved
  • After purchase, the system automatically creates your enrolment record, generates an official invoice, and emails it to you
  • You then receive a secure registration link — a unique, time-limited link that carries you into the next room
02
The Student Portal
The campus office · Everything student-related lives here
Think of the Student Portal as the main office of a university reception centre. This is where you manage your identity, finances, communications, and support — and where you return every time you need something from the Academy.
  • Identity card — Your Student ID, Student Code, and wallet number (masked by default, revealed with the eye icon)
  • RFX wallet & credit — Prize money and referral rewards land here. Credit can buy courses, mentorship, seat transfers, or merch
  • Mailbox — Every official notice: invoices, registration links, announcements. Downloadable as files
  • Referral link — Share the Academy, earn commission when your friend is fully enrolled
  • Live support — Sarrah answers around the clock. When a human is needed, the team takes over
  • Trust & standing — Your Trust Bar reflects your conduct. It rises with good behaviour, falls with breaches, and is always reviewed by a moderator
03
RFX OS Academy
The classroom · Where you learn
RFX OS is the Academy itself — lessons, progress, Intelligent Assessments, certificates. It is the most protected room in the building because it holds the value of your education.
  • 13 chapters across three difficulty lanes — Standard, Challenging, Elite
  • Intelligent Assessments — machine-graded, measuring discipline and competence, not luck
  • Simulated trading — real markets, real decisions, zero real money at risk
  • Workshops & challenges — build strategies, tune them, break them, learn by doing
  • Trade journaling & performance tracking — evidence of your progress, kept forever
  • Watermarked pages — your Student ID is on every page. Copying is blocked. Printing is a privilege earned by trusted students
🔒 One student, one account, one active session

Signing in on a new device automatically ends your previous session. No need to sign out of old devices — the system does it for you. This protects the value of the education you paid for — for you and every student after you.

02 The Registration Gate

Between the Front Desk and the Student Portal stands a registration gate — deliberately, so the Academy knows every single person before they touch any material.

1
Secure link
Your registration email's link is unique, single-use and time-limited. An expired or used link is refused and a fresh one is issued.
2
Identity
Name, surname, email, country, phone, address, date of birth — and a verification selfie, captured from camera, mobile or files.
3
Human check
Email verification with a 6-digit code, plus CAPTCHA. A machine confirms the basics — a person stays part of the review.
4
Agreements
The exact version of the Terms, Conditions and Fair Usage Policy is recorded, with the exact time of acceptance.
5
Approval
Staff verify the registration is tied to a genuine paid enrolment. They approve — then a Student ID and Student Code are created.
6
Handshake
Your approved identity is handed to RFX OS. The Academy confirms receipt — so a network hiccup never creates two identities for one student.
🛡️ Until this process completes

You hold a demo / under-review profile — never full access. Registration links are tracked, verification codes are brute-force protected, and a failed registration can be re-applied for or resolved to credit or refund — all automated, all recorded.
