      One-click full audit + the mechanic

      Student complaining? Press this. It re-proves the whole chain live — payment → registration → approval → handoff, plus money reconciliation, identity integrity, store health and every security guard — then reports PASS/FAIL. Scratch records are removed; nothing it audits is changed. If something fails, pass the screwdriver to the mechanic — it auto-repairs what is safely machine-fixable, logs every change, and re-proves the chain. Money and human decisions are never auto-touched.

      What does the audit actually check? (like walking the electrical fence — press to expand)
      Think of it as walking the whole perimeter of the property with a voltmeter — every few minutes, automatically, whether anyone is looking:
      • The full journey, proven live: it creates a scratch purchase and pushes it through the real pipeline — payment webhook → enrollment → invoice → registration → approval → handshake → ACTIVE — then deletes the scratch. If every stage passes, the chain that students ride works end to end.
      • Money reconciles: every wallet ledger is summed and matched against the stored balance, to the cent. No negative balances, no orphaned credit, every rand accounted for.
      • Identity integrity: every ACTIVE student holds a real Student ID + Code, no code is ever duplicated, and the Trust Bar ledgers reconcile — the bar a moderator sees is a true record, not a drifted number.
      • Store health: a real write→read round-trip proves persistence works and the multi-tab revision guard is alive (a stale tab can never clobber newer data).
      • Capacity headroom: how much of the store is used and how many more students fit — honest room-to-grow, never a guess.
      • The security guards, re-attacked: the same 5 self-tests that simulate an intruder — brute-forcing logins, guessing verification codes, reusing an expired link, checking selfies are purged after decisions, and proving the one-session rule. All defended, all the time.
      If any check fails, the mechanic (one button below) fixes what is safely machine-fixable, logs every change with the operator's name, and re-proves the chain. Money, approvals and human decisions are never auto-touched — those always wait for a person. That is the whole point: you are the eyes, the machine is the fence.
