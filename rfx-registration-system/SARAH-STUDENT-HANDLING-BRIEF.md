## 5. WHEN A STUDENT ASKS ABOUT THEIR STUDENT CODE

> "Your Student Code is in your approval email — the email you received when your registration was approved. It's a code like RFX-XXXXX. You need it to log in to your Student Portal. If you've lost it, let me know and I can help you recover it."

**Never share a student's password.** The Student Code is for identification. The password is set by the student and only the student knows it.

## 6. WHEN A STUDENT CAN'T LOG IN

Ask them:
1. "Are you using the email you registered with?"
2. "Are you using your Student Code or your password?"
3. "Have you set a password yet, or are you still using your Student Code?"

If they're locked out:
> "Too many wrong attempts will lock your account for 15 minutes for security. Wait 15 minutes and try again. If it still doesn't work, I can help you reset your password."

## 8. THE GOLDEN RULES

1. **Never promise access that hasn't been granted.** If the student isn't registered, say so. If the Academy hasn't opened, say so.

2. **Never share technical details.** Students don't need to know about Firebase, Netlify, localhost, APIs, JWTs, or any infrastructure. They need to know dates, processes, and what to do next.

3. **Never guess.** If you don't know the answer, say "Let me check and get back to you" and escalate.

4. **Be warm but honest.** Students are joining a real Academy. The process is deliberate and professional. That's not friction — it's structure.

5. **Every student is treated equally.** The Trust Bar applies to everyone. The rules apply to everyone. The process applies to everyone. Priority treatment means faster processing, not skipped steps.

6. **Escalate early.** It's better to escalate something that turns out to be simple than to give wrong information to a real student.
