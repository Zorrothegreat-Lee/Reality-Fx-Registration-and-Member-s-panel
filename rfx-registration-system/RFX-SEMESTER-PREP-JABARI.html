07 The Rules That Keep Everyone Safe
  • One student, one account — sharing or selling access is a breach of the Fair Usage Policy
  • One active session — the moment you sign in anywhere new, the previous session ends automatically. No need to sign out of old devices; the system does it for you
  • Intelligent Assessments are monitored — unusual timing or perfect-score patterns are reviewed by the moderator, who checks evidence before any decision
  • Refunds end access — a refund request revokes all material rights and starts a re-application cooldown. The full policy is in the agreements you accepted
  • Your Trust Bar reflects your standing — good conduct raises it; breaches lower it. Every adjustment is recorded and visible to you
🛡️ Why these measures exist

Every safeguard — session tracking, watermarking, security event logging, the Trust Bar — exists so your profile never stops working, your login never fails, and the Academy is never compromised. They are not for show. If a measure ever feels like extra fuss, it is the thing standing between you and a day when your account stops working for no reason you can see.

Reality FX would rather carry a student who is struggling than keep staff who do not meet the standard. That is not a slogan — it is how the system is built.

  • Staff are held to a stricter standard — a team member whose standing falls too far is placed under review, because the people who run the Academy must live up to the quality it promises.
